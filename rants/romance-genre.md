thesis idea:
the mainstream interpretation of the narrative genre of romance is extremely flawed. for the modern individual, romance is a bonus or an addition to a well-lived life, not the point of it. in addition, romance is inherently trapped within its own premise due to restrictive nature of its obligatory conventions and scenes, which artificially block progression or otherwise ruin what humans ought to read romance for. if stories are meant to teach people, then romance fails because its too dramatic - if stories are meant to entertain prople, then romance fails because its too narratively restricted
