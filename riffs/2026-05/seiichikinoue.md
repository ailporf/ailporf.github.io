One of my favourite short romcom manga, *No Matter What You Say, Furi-san is Scary!*, is written by this mangaka named Seiichi Kinoue.
<br> They're a relatively unknown mangaka who hasn't written anything else but some shorts in manga anthologies. They are apparently serialising another work on Comic Walker (based on info from a Twitter post in July 2023), but their last Twitter post was in April 2024.

I feel like I'm genuinely looking at a Spotify artist with 400 monthly listeners while I'm stalking this random mangaka, but I seriously love their artstyle and tight pacing.
<br> It's not even a masterpiece, but it is undoubtedly one of my favourite works of all time.
