*Marriage Toxin* seems like it would be great, and honestly a huge draw for me is Mei - the crossdressing "guy" character. <br>
However, since it's a mainline action shonen, I'm extremely sceptical about the potential paths the story could take.

Of course, the obvious and easiest path is that the MC Gero and Mei will remain purely platonic the entire story. This would be fine - but also boring. <br>
The second path is the worst path; if Mei suddenly reveals near the climax, "Aha! I'm actually a cisgender woman *pretending* to be a cis man who crossdresses so you wouldn't fall in love with me / me with you!" that would be the biggest, most disappointing cop-out of all time. <br>
The third and most exciting path is if Mei and Gero end up in an actual yaoi relationship. This would frankly be revolutionary, and I'd love it, but it's also just... not looking likely. <br>
We'll see when I start reading it (even though I have a rule to not start reading ongoing manga).
