Often I struggle with characters.
<br> I always think they're simple but they are probably the hardest thing in all of writing to do (other than pacing management). 
Getting them to talk and think believably is difficult as all hell. Why does x talk to y? *They're the only thing there is to talk to, I'd talk to them too!* Is the audience or me satisfied with that answer? 
<br> Do they (the character) need to want something from the other character? If they dont, is it bad writing?

TL;DR don't listen to writing advice like gospel, treat it like a test parameter.
