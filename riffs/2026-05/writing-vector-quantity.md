The more i write, the more I realise the advice to "just write" becomes completely useless. You NEED direction to have a vector. Writing is not a scalar quantity. 
<br> At least, for me. Some people just have that intuitive grasp of prose to be able to write without structure (I am not one of those people). Or they just write volume to make up for lack of intentional practice, which is just too slow for me.
