Storytelling is inherently a discipline meant to be shared amongst people, which is funny cause the modern version of storytelling has decoupled itself from that social aspect. When a modern writer writes, they must write selfishly, I think. 
<br> I mean, you must be selfish in most circumstances, but specifically for writing you should not write for the general audience, you should write for yourself. 
<br> Let's just hope that you are a harsh critic so you don't do things that make no sense.
