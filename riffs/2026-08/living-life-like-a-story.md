People think of stories like real life, and real life like a stories. We are overlapping them way too much. We need to forgive each other for our mistakes, and we need to accept that stories are NOT real life.

I used to think living your life like it was a story was good, now I think its a terrible way to live.  
I used to think the moral of my life was that nothing mattered, as depressing as that sounds.  
More accurately, when something bad happened and I couldn't find a fault in myself or someone else (usually the former), I'd just think "oh, the moral of this story is that sometimes things happen for no reason." - but that's just rationalising and retrofitting a moral to reality itself. 

*Reality* has no inherent meaning, which means we shouldn't pretend its meaning is that there is no meaning either.
